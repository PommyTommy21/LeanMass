Programme Philosophy

  • Train close to failure. Final set of every exercise: stop with 0–2 reps left. Compounds 1–2 reps in reserve, isolation 0–1.
  • Rep ranges: heavy compounds 6–8, most work 8–12, isolation and delts 12–20. Anything from about 5 to 30 reps grows muscle if it's hard enough.
  • Double progression: hit the top of the rep range on every set, then add the smallest weight jump. Log every session.
  • Stretch under load. Fly, incline curl, and overhead extension are in because muscles grow well when loaded in the lengthened position. Lower slowly, don't bounce.
  • Compounds first, isolation after. Session length 60–80 min.
  • Rest: 2–4 min on heavy compounds, 60–90 sec on isolation. Short rests on big lifts cost you reps and growth.
  • Warm-up: 5 min easy cardio, then 2–3 ramp-up sets on your first lift only. Not every exercise needs it.
  • Tap any exercise to see the 3D form guide.

How To Run It

  • Weeks 1–2: use the first number of sets if an exercise is new, and leave 2 reps in the tank. Learn the movements.
  • Weeks 3–8: push every working set to 0–2 reps from failure and chase more reps or weight each week.
  • Week 9 (deload): same exercises, half the sets, lighter weights. Then restart with the new weights. Deload sooner if lifts drop for 2 weeks in a row, joints ache or sleep falls apart.
  • Missed a day? Pick up where you left off, don't double up sessions.

Your weight has changed since the last version, so expect to retune once or twice in the first month.

Best Protein Sources

  • Chicken breast / thigh — 30–35g protein per 150g serving
  • Eggs + egg whites — easy, versatile, complete amino acid profile
  • Greek yogurt / cottage cheese — casein protein, great before bed
  • White fish (cod, tilapia, tuna) — lean, high protein, easy to prep
  • Red meat (mince, steak) — 2–3x per week for creatine + iron
  • Whey protein shake — 1 scoop if hitting 180g from food is hard

Meal Timing

  • Pre-workout (1–2 hrs before): carb-heavy — rice, pasta, oats + protein
  • Post-workout: protein + carbs within a couple of hours — shake + banana, chicken + white rice
  • Before bed: slow protein — Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, or casein
  • Through the day: spread protein over 4–5 meals, about 35–50g each
  • Hydration: 3–4 litres of water per day on training days
  • Struggling to eat 3,200? Drink some calories (milk, smoothies with oats and peanut butter) and add olive oil or nuts to meals.

Supplements (Minimal & Effective)

  • Creatine Monohydrate — 5g/day: the best-studied supplement for size and strength. Take it daily, timing doesn't matter, no loading needed.
  • Whey Protein: only to help hit protein. It's convenient food, not magic.
  • Vitamin D3: useful if you get little sun, especially in winter.
  • Omega-3 Fish Oil: optional. Fine if you don't eat much fish.
Rules & Tips
The things that actually separate progress from plateaus

Double Progression — Your #1 Rule

1
Pick a weight that gets you into the bottom of the rep range with 0–2 reps left on your last set.
2
Add reps each week until every set hits the top of the range (e.g. 3×12 at the same weight).
3
Then add the smallest jump — 2.5 kg upper body, 5 kg lower. Reps drop back to the bottom of the range and you climb again.
4
Log every session — weight, reps, and how close to failure you were. If you can't beat last week, check sleep and food before changing the plan.
🎯
Effort is the multiplier
Sets only count if they're hard. Stopping 4–5 reps short on everything is the most common reason people stall.
😴
Sleep is non-negotiable
You grow while you recover. Aim for 8–9 hours. Bad sleep drops performance and recovery more than any supplement can fix.
📋
Track your food
Use MyFitnessPal or Cronometer for at least 4 weeks. Most people overestimate how much they eat.
📏
Track your body
Weigh every morning after the bathroom and use the weekly average. Take monthly photos and measure arms, chest and thighs.
⚠️
Form before weight
Never sacrifice form for ego, especially presses and rows. Controlled reps with a full stretch grow more than sloppy heavy ones.
🔄
Deload every 6–8 weeks
Cut sets in half for one week. You'll come back stronger and your joints will thank you. It's not lost progress.
🏃
Easy cardio is fine
A 20–30 min walk on rest days helps recovery and appetite without eating into gains.
📅
Run it 12+ weeks
Muscle takes months, not weeks. The best programme is the one you execute consistently.
